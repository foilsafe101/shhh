## What Shhh is
A web map of places with little or no anthropogenic sound, built from
accumulated aircraft flight paths plus road and rail data. Leaflet frontend,
flight data from the OpenSky Network's Trino database, quiet-zone overlay
computed by generate_quiet_overlay.py.

- Live map: https://foilsafe101.github.io/shhh/ (GitHub Pages from /docs)
- Repo: https://github.com/foilsafe101/shhh, branch main
- Local checkout: /Users/Shared/shhh on Mark's MacBook Air (the "Black Mac").
  Python venv at .venv. Two macOS users: foil (owns the .git files) and
  markustribus (admin, the one Mark types as).
- The Mac mini has no copy of the project.
